Accessibility

This page is about the website at aibhlinn.ai. We want the website to be calm and easy to use for everyone.

What we have done

  • The site runs no scripts, so every page works with scripts turned off.
  • Nothing moves or plays without you clicking a button, and nothing pops up or times out. Our video, on the services page, starts only when you press play. There is no sound, and the page says in words what is shown.
  • Pages use real headings and landmarks, and the main pages start with a "Skip to content" link.
  • A clear outline shows which link or button has keyboard focus.
  • The site follows your device's light or dark setting.
  • The site works with Windows contrast themes.
  • Text reflows on a screen 320 pixels wide and when zoomed to 200%, with no sideways scrolling.
  • Text contrast is at least 6.9 to 1 in both light and dark.
  • Links inside text are underlined, so colour is never the only sign.
  • Contact is via email, and every email address is written out so the address can be copied.
  • The contact section says how to say our name, and the brand kit says how to write our name.

What is not perfect

  • The name at the top of the home page is an image, with a text alternative.
  • The logo files in the brand kit are images, each with a text description.
  • Our examples on our services page are screenshots and a video. Each has a text description, but the words inside a screenshot are part of the picture and get small on a phone. Each screenshot has a link that opens the screenshot at full size.
  • This site has been checked in Chrome at phone, tablet and desktop widths, in light, dark and contrast themes. The site has not been independently audited.

Tell us

If something on this site is hard to use, email hello@aibhlinn.ai with Accessibility in the subject.

No ads. No tracking. No selling your data. Ever.
