Questions

Is Pie Timers really free?

Yes. Everything that works on one device is free, permanently: the timers, your weekly schedule, appointments, the calculator, and alerts while the app is open.

Do I need an account?

No. Pie Timers works without one, and offline. An account is only needed to sync between devices and to get alerts when the app is closed. You sign in with a one-time code sent to your email, or with Google, so there is no password to create.

What does Premium add?

Sync between devices, a read-only calendar connection, and alerts when the app is closed. Every new account gets a free trial. No card is required, and nothing is charged automatically when the trial ends. See pricing.

Who do I pay, and can I get a refund?

If you subscribe on the web, you buy from Paddle, our payment provider, and your receipt will show Paddle. You can cancel at any time. If the app has not worked for you, write to support@aibhlinn.ai within 30 days and we will ask Paddle to refund you. The Pie Timers Refund Policy has the details.

Do you show ads or track me?

No ads and no third-party trackers, and we never sell your data. This website sets no cookies and has no analytics. Pie Timers counts the first time it opens, in a browser or in the Android app, as a daily total with no name, account or ID attached. Like any web request, that one reaches our database provider with your IP address. The Pie Timers Privacy Policy explains how.

Is Pie Timers a medical tool?

No. It is a visual aid. It is not a medical device, a clinical tool or a substitute for professional advice, and it should not be your only reminder for anything that matters.

How we build

Our promise

No ads. No tracking. Ever.

We never sell your data or share it for marketing. Each time we publish, an automatic check stops the release if a known advertising or tracking service has crept into the code. This website runs no scripts, sets no cookies and loads nothing from anyone else.

Free where it counts

Everything that works on one device is free, permanently. Paying adds sync and extras on top.

No guilt

Our apps never scold you for a bad day. The pie simply empties, and tomorrow there is another one.
