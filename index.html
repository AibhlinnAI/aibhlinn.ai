Neuro-affirming event support

For conferences and other events we put your event program online as a live countdown visual timer. Your program shows on the big screens at your venue, and attendees open the same program on their own phones from a QR code. They can check what is on, what is next and how long is left, without having to ask or rustle through an event pack.

Pie Timers Q&A

What is Pie Timers, and who makes the app?

Pie Timers is a countdown visual timer that lets you know where you are in your day, without doing any sums. Our timers are shaped like a pie that gets eaten as time passes. One glance confirms for you the time elapsed since you began and time remaining to completion.

AibhlínnAI has developed Pie Timers to be a permanently free app when used on one device. An AibhlínnAI Premium subscription unlocks sync across all your devices, as well as some other premium features.

You can access Pie Timers in your browser, at pietimers.aibhlinn.ai, or as an Android app on Google Play.

Is Pie Timers really free?

Yes. Everything that works on one device is free, permanently: the timers, your weekly schedule, appointments, the calculator, and alerts while the app is open.

Do I need to sign in to an account to use Pie Timers?

No. Pie Timers works without one, and offline. An account is only needed to sync between devices and to get alerts when the app is closed. You sign in with a one-time code sent to your email, or with Google, so there is no password to create.

What does an AibhlínnAI Premium subscription add?

Sync between devices, a read-only calendar connection, and alerts when the app is closed. Every new account gets a free trial. No card is required, and nothing is charged automatically when the trial ends. See pricing.

Who do I pay for an AibhlínnAI Premium subscription, and can I get a refund?

If you subscribe on the web, you buy from Paddle, our payment provider, and your receipt will show Paddle. You can cancel at any time. If you change your mind, write to support@aibhlinn.ai within 30 days of a payment and we will ask Paddle to refund that payment, less the fee Paddle keeps. If the app has a fault, your refund is paid in full. The AibhlínnAI Premium Refund Policy has the details.

Is Pie Timers a medical tool?

No. Pie Timers is a visual aid. Pie Timers is not a:

  • medical device
  • clinical tool, or
  • substitute for professional advice.

Pie Timers should never be your only reminder for any events that have consequences from not being met.
