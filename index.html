Pie Timers

Some days you eat the pie. Some days the pie eats you.

Pie Timers shows how much of your day is left as a shrinking pie. One glance tells you where you are, without doing the sums.

  • Free on one device, permanently. Timers, your weekly schedule, appointments and the calculator.
  • Runs in your browser. Nothing to install. You can add it to your home screen if you like.
  • Premium adds the extras. Sync between devices, calendar connection and alerts when the app is closed.
  • Android app now in testing. Open Pie Timers on an Android phone to join the testing list.

Our promise

No ads. No tracking. Ever.

We never sell your data or share it for marketing. Each time we publish, an automatic check stops the release if a known advertising or tracking service has crept into the code.

Free where it counts

Everything that works on one device is free, permanently. Paying adds sync and extras on top.

No guilt

Our apps never scold you for a bad day. The pie simply empties, and tomorrow there is another one.
