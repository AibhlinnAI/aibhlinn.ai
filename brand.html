The name

Say it: AYV-leen A-I

Write the name as AibhlínnAI: one word, mixed case, never all capitals. If you cannot type the accent above the second i, AibhlinnAI is fine in bodies of text. Titles, headings and anywhere else the name is on show always keep the accent.

To type the í:

  • Windows: hold Alt and type 161 on the number pad, with Num Lock on. The number row along the top does not work.
  • Mac: hold Option and press E, then press I.
  • Phone or tablet: press and hold the letter I, then pick í.

About AibhlínnAI, to copy

AibhlínnAI is a small independent AI studio from South Australia. AibhlínnAI builds simple tools to support busy minds, starting with Pie Timers, and provides guidance to organisations to make room for neurodivergent people. AibhlínnAI offers neuro-affirming event support, AI and automation consultancy, and neurodivergence advocacy. The AibhlínnAI tagline is: Aim for the gap. Simple tools to support busy minds.
