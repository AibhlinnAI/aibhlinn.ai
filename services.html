How an automation and AI project runs

AibhlínnAI gives organisations practical help finding the work that automation can take on. We start with a small pilot, so you can see whether it helps before anything is rolled out.

  1. Find the work

    You tell us about the tasks that take up time in your organisation. Together we choose one that is small, repeated and easy to check.

  2. Run a small pilot

    We set up a pilot for that one task. The rest of your work carries on as it is.

  3. See whether it helps

    You see the pilot working on that task. We tell you plainly what worked and what did not.

  4. You decide

    Nothing is rolled out until you have seen the pilot and chosen to go on. If the pilot did not help, we say so.

Privacy and risk

  • Before a pilot starts, we agree with you what information it will use.
  • We tell you which services will handle that information, by name. Our own privacy notice does the same.
  • Where we can, a pilot starts with sample data, not real records about people.
  • We design each pilot so that a person checks the result.

What you get

  • A pilot on one task, for you to try.
  • A plain language explanation of what the pilot does, what it does not do, and what it needs from your people.
  • A straight answer on whether it is worth going further.
  • No promise of savings in advance. The pilot is there to show you.

We will tell you when automation is the wrong tool. The home page lists what suits automation, and what does not.
