What we do

Apps

Our first app is Pie Timers. Pie Timers is a countdown visual timer that lets you know where you are in your day, without doing any sums. Our timers are shaped like a pie that gets eaten as time passes. One glance confirms for you the time elapsed since you began and time remaining to completion. Pie Timers is free on one device, permanently, and runs in your browser or as an Android app on Google Play. AibhlínnAI Premium adds sync between devices, a read-only calendar connection and alerts when the app is closed.

Neuro-affirming event support

For conferences and other events we put your event program online as a live countdown visual timer. Your program shows on the big screens at your venue, and attendees open the same program on their own phones from a QR code. As our online phone programs are hosted on a web browser, there is nothing to install and no account to make. For any attendee who would like to learn more about us, links to the AibhlínnAI and Pie Timers websites will be included in our online phone programs. We can also help you arrange planned breaks, times when a quiet space is open, and a printable program in plain language.
