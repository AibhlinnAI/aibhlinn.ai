Privacy notice for this website

Last updated: 22 November 2026
See also the website terms and the Pie Timers Privacy Policy.

The short version

  • This website is plain pages. It runs no scripts, sets no cookies, and has no analytics, no ads, no forms and no accounts.
  • GitHub hosts the pages. Like any web host, it records your IP address and browser in its standard web server logs.
  • If you email us, we keep your email so we can answer it.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for marketing.

1. What this notice covers

This notice covers the website at aibhlinn.ai. The Pie Timers Privacy Policy is separate. It covers the app, accounts, sync and payments.

2. Who is responsible

AibhlínnAI, ABN 70 810 791 817, of South Australia, Australia, is responsible for the personal information described here. Contact: hello@aibhlinn.ai.

3. When you visit

Every page, image and font on this website comes from aibhlinn.ai. Nothing is loaded from any other company: no advertising, no social media buttons and no embedded content.

The pages are served by GitHub Pages. GitHub receives your IP address and browser, in standard web server logs, and handles them overseas.

This website sets no cookies and saves nothing in your browser's storage. As with any website, your browser may keep copies of the pages and images for a while so they load faster.

4. Links to other places

This website links to Pie Timers, Google Play, Instagram, Facebook, Discord and GitHub, and this page links to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner. They are ordinary links. Nothing from those places loads until you choose to follow one, and then their own privacy policies apply. Our pages ask your browser not to tell the other site which page you came from.

5. If you email us

If you email us, Cloudflare receives your message and passes it on to our mailbox with Google, which holds it overseas. Our replies are sent through Resend, and pass through their systems overseas.

We use an AI assistant, Claude, made by Anthropic, to help us sort our email and draft replies. So the assistant reads the email you send us, and Anthropic processes it overseas. A person reads and sends every reply.

We keep your email, and our reply, in our mailbox until you ask us to delete them.

6. Who else is involved

  • GitHub (GitHub Pages) serves these pages to your device. It gets your IP address and browser, in standard web server logs.
  • Cloudflare receives email sent to an @aibhlinn.ai address and passes it on to our mailbox.
  • Google holds our mailbox, so it holds any email you send us and any email we send you.
  • Resend sends our email to you, so it gets your email address and our replies.
  • Anthropic makes Claude, the AI assistant that helps us sort our email, so it processes any email you send us.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information to anybody.

7. Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, and ask us to correct it or delete it. Email hello@aibhlinn.ai and we will respond within 30 days.

In Australia you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

8. Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it.

No ads. No tracking. No selling your data. Ever.

This site sets no cookies and has no analytics.
